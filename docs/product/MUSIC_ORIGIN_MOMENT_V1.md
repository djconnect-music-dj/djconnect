## Product and contract

Menu question: **Waar ken ik dit nummer van?** Native type `music_origin`; knowledge intent `music_origin_story`; versioned payload `music_origin.schema_version = 1`. Retain the original track title/artist and one relation with explicit direction, related recording/work/media and uncertainty (`stated`, `likely`, `possible`). Supported relations: sample, interpolation, cover, remake, remix, earlier performance, media use, shared source and influence. A media use does not invent a second song. Optional artwork is never a success gate. Opening an original is discovery, never implicit playback.

Reuse `ask_dj/moments`, request/status/cancel and original UUIDv7 journal. Existing Profile/Session/provider/revocation guards fence every await; the captured subject survives an ordinary track change. Direct durable commit precedes presentation, crash recovery and the roughly20-second avatar timer. One Moment ID; no duplicate chat/history write. Low/music_only permits explicit work without changing settings or enabling proactive selection.

## Best-effort research

Extend the existing public_research owner with websearch-first music-origin discovery. Only bounded public title/artist text leaves Core. Reuse its HTTPS/DNS/redirect/robots/SSRF/byte/time protection. Source pages are untrusted data. Read promising pages where available; distinguish a retrieved page from a search excerpt. No mandatory Wikipedia, master ID, MusicBrainz/SecondHandSongs identity, two-source quota, evidence-per-sentence or extra evaluator call. One usable, uncertain finding can be a lasting rich Moment. Never fabricate a source visit, relationship or listener memory. No finding and a temporary source failure remain distinct friendly outcomes.

Budget: one search query, at most eight shared HTTP requests including robots/redirects, at most two selected public sources,512KiB per response,20-second research window and one narration call within the original90-second operation. Source reuse obeys original expiry. No WhoSampled scraping, account access, extra provider activation or audio fingerprinting.
