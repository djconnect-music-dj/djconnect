## Stable normal genre publication

An ordinary Music DNA publication whose newly calculated favorite-genre list
is exactly unchanged retains the original committed list object. Changed
values still publish a new container. This preserves the existing complete
store/row/container identity, generation, safety epoch, normalized genre and
terminal withdrawal fences. An unobserved A-to-B-to-A value change must still
retire the original offer; privacy/off/clear/save failure/source replacement
must still retire it. No new lease or visible budget is created. This repairs
the demonstrated no-op producer update, not unproved causes of every Horizon
or For-you withdrawal.

## Current availability is not a promise of a research result

Inventory GET remains a pure read of the original loaded owner, Session,
provider, captured subject and already collected evidence. It never polls a
feed, registers a source, invokes a model or consumes a story reservation.
The existing `subject_ref` and original context/expiry remain authoritative.

For DJ Bulletin, producer presence alone is insufficient. Availability must
also inspect the existing collector and reservation state. A fresh, eligible
candidate may be used only in its original context. Otherwise the same pure
selection rules used by preparation must find two fresh, distinct publishers
whose stories have not already been reserved. Current source failures and
uninitialized collector state are distinguished from a successful empty
collection or exhausted original story reservations.

A private headline bulletin prepared in the background can serve an explicit
request from the same owner and Session even when the request captures a
different provider tuple. The original preparation and request authorities
both remain valid, and taking the original candidate preserves both source
deadlines and consumes it once. Model-generated candidates still require the
same provider identity.

Additive inventory reason codes are `bulletin_sources_pending`,
`bulletin_sources_temporarily_unavailable`, `bulletin_not_enough_fresh_sources`
and `bulletin_no_new_stories`. Their five-language copy describes current news
availability. An explicit request rechecks current evidence and returns the
corresponding bounded result if eligibility changed after inventory. It must
not describe this as unsuccessful artist/subject research. Existing request
states and once-only operation ownership remain unchanged.

Keep original publication dates, the two-hour source window, both-clock
deadlines, fixed private headline sources, source rights and the24-hour global
anti-repeat reservations. No reservation deletion, lease extension, repeated
model generation, substitute publisher, extra article fetch, shared publication
or new permission is introduced to make a row available.

## Ephemeral unavailable-row presentation

A consumer may mark only the matching original inventory/request/type/subject
and context unavailable after its terminal source-unavailability result.
This is RAM-only presentation, not durable evidence or an authority decision.
Fresh inventory replaces that mask; original expiry, owner/Session/source
change and context withdrawal clear it. It cannot cross a new subject or
operation, suppress a real fresh result or conceal a known artist-identity bug.
Core continues to make and recheck the actual availability decision.

## Artist stories use actual credited artist identities

Inventory may include an optional top-level `subject_context` with exactly
`subject_ref` and known `track_title`, `artist`, `album` strings. Missing or
invalid values are omitted, never replaced by action labels or an inferred
artist. Text is at most512 characters without control characters. It is read
only from this same original capture's provider catalogue and safe playback
labels; album comes only from the exact captured catalogue's `album_name`.
It cannot use a later track, create a new deadline/permission or transfer the
original subject reference. Consumers render this DATA once above the cards,
not by deriving an album name from an album-story action tile.

Preserve bounded `{id, name}` artist pairs directly from the observed provider
track. Joined display labels are presentation only: never split commas,
ampersands, feature/remix wording or a band name into invented identities.
An artist story must not require the recording/remix title to match a public
recording before a genuine credited artist can be researched.

Within the original guarded source budget, bounded identity discovery may
consider at most four actual provider credits in their original order. It
selects one reciprocally qualified public artist before narrative generation.
No fame-based or hardcoded artist selection, recording-credit inference or
fallback to another subject after generation/assessment is allowed. The
original track, selected credit, provider IDs, public source identity and
owner/Session/source deadline remain joined throughout the request. Missing
structured multi-artist evidence fails closed; it does not authorize guessing.

Transport/rate/source failures remain temporary failures. Genuine ambiguity,
unsupported identity and insufficient cited material remain distinguishable
from successful research. Existing recording/work/producer evidence, privacy,
manual request ownership and model budgets are not relaxed.

## Required causal qualification

Cover a fresh pair, one-source-only freshness, an already-reserved sole source,
feed failure/backoff, pending startup, cached candidate withdrawal and a race
between inventory and request. Inventory performs zero IO/model/reservation
writes. Preserve all existing anti-repeat, rights, expiry and manual/private
speech cases. Exercise five-language inventory and terminal result copy.

Artist regressions include two real structured credit fixtures with a remix
title, a band whose name contains a comma/ampersand, multiple unrelated public
identities, missing/misaligned IDs, a temporary source failure and withdrawal
at every awaited identity/research boundary. A qualified credited artist story
must succeed without a recording identity; recording/work assertions must
still fail when that identity is unavailable. Software fixtures are not real
publisher, model, installed or physical acceptance.
